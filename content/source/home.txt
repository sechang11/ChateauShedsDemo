Chateau Sheds | Custom Sheds, Garages, Metal Buildings & Outdoor Structures in Massachusetts

Chateau Sheds designs and delivers high-performance outdoor structures built specifically for New England weather conditions. Based in Granby, MA and serving customers throughout Massachusetts and across New England, we specialize in custom sheds, garages, metal buildings, animal shelters, greenhouses, and outdoor saunas engineered for durability, functionality, and long-term structural integrity.
