Old Hickory Double Wide Utility Buildings – Large Open-Span Structures Built for Expansion

Chateau Sheds offers Old Hickory Double Wide Utility Buildings designed for customers who need more than a traditional shed footprint. Available in 20-foot and 24-foot widths and lengths up to 40 feet, these structures are delivered in two fully built sections and joined on-site to create one expansive interior space. Whether you are expanding storage capacity, building a multi-vehicle garage, creating a workshop, or planning a future livable conversion, double wide utility buildings provide a scalable solution without starting from scratch.Wide-Span Interior Design Without Interior Support Posts

Double Wide Utility Buildings are engineered as two single-slope units with framed high-wall openings that align during installation. Once connected, the dividing wall is eliminated, creating a broad, uninterrupted interior layout.

This design allows for flexible floor planning, vehicle storage, racking systems, workshop setups, or open recreational space without structural obstructions limiting your layout.A Practical Alternative to Site-Built Construction

For many customers, a double wide building provides a faster and more cost-controlled alternative to traditional stick-built garages or additions. Delivered pre-constructed in two halves and assembled on a prepared pad, these structures reduce build timelines while still offering substantial square footage.

Common uses include:

• Multi-vehicle garages
• Contractor workshops
• Equipment storage facilities
• Hobby studios
• Large she sheds or man caves
• Airbnb-ready shell structures
• Future livable units

The ability to customize door and window placement allows the structure to evolve with your needs.Structural Floor System & Pad Requirements

Double wide buildings must be installed on a properly prepared, level gravel or concrete pad with continuous skid support. The reinforced floor system is designed to accommodate vehicle weight, equipment loads, and workshop activity when supported correctly.

Because the building is delivered in two sections, proper foundation preparation ensures alignment, structural integrity, and long-term performance.Custom Garage Packages, Layout Options & Exterior Finishes

Old Hickory Double Wide Buildings can be configured with:

• Roll-up garage doors
• House-style entry doors
• Double barn doors
• Window packages
• Custom paint or urethane stain finishes
• Secondary trim color options
