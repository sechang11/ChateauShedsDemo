Many Old Hickory sheds include screened windows to improve ventilation while keeping insects out. If you need to remove the screens for cleaning, replacement, or seasonal airflow, the process is simple and requires minimal tools.

Start by opening the shed window fully to access the interior screen frame. Most Old Hickory window screens are secured with spring clips or small retaining tabs. Gently compress the clips or release the tabs and pull the screen frame inward toward you. Avoid forcing the frame, as excessive pressure can bend the aluminum edging.

Once removed, inspect the screen mesh and frame for debris or damage. Clean with mild soap and water, allow to dry completely, and reinstall by reversing the process, ensuring the frame sits flush and secure within the window track.
