Custom Outdoor Wood-Fired Finnish Saunas by North Country Saunas

Chateau Sheds proudly offers custom outdoor wood-fired Finnish-style saunas built by North Country Saunas. These traditional heat systems are centered around authentic wood-burning stoves surrounded by granite sauna rocks, allowing both dry heat sessions and steam-style experiences. Designed through one-on-one consultation, our saunas are built to match your space, style, and usage goals. With Amish craftsmanship, a proprietary 7-layer wall system, and multiple upgrade options, these outdoor saunas are engineered for performance, comfort, and long-term structural integrity.Traditional Wood-Fired Heat & Proven Sauna Benefits

Wood-fired Finnish saunas are known for delivering high-temperature heat designed to promote deep sweating and full-body warmth. Many users incorporate sauna sessions into their routine for:

• Muscle relaxation after physical activity
• Circulation support
• Stress reduction and mental reset
• Post-workday decompression
• Heat conditioning routines

The granite rock stove system allows you to control the experience. Enjoy a dry sauna session or introduce water onto the heated stones to create steam for a traditional wet sauna environment.Seven-Layer Wall System for Heat Retention & Structural Performance

What sets our outdoor saunas apart is the 7-layer wall system engineered specifically for exterior installations. This layered construction includes:

• Exterior siding (log siding, tongue & groove pine, or LP Smart Panel)
• Weather barrier protection
• Structural framing
• Full insulation
• Harvia vapor barrier
• Airflow strapping system
• Interior cedar finish

This multi-layer approach improves heat retention, controls moisture movement, and enhances long-term durability. The result is a sauna that heats efficiently and maintains consistent interior temperatures session after session.Fully Customizable Design with Premium Upgrade Options

Most customers choose to design their sauna during an in-person consultation. We help you determine the ideal size, bench layout, door placement, and window configuration to suit your intended use.

Customization options include:

• White cedar, red cedar, or blended interior finishes
• Change room upgrades for added privacy and convenience
• Covered porch extensions for cooling between sessions
• Off-grid LED lighting packages
• Metal roofing with custom paint or stain colors
• Multiple wood stove sizes or electric-only configurations

Every sauna is built to match your property and usage goals rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.Amish Craftsmanship, On-Lot Models & East Coast Delivery

Our wood-fired saunas are Amish-built using heavy-duty framing systems and traditional construction techniques. We maintain multiple sauna models on-site for customers to walk through, inspect, and experience before placing an order.

Financing is available, and we provide professional delivery throughout New England, New Jersey, and along the East Coast to Florida.
